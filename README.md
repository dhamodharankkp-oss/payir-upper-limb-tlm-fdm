## Overview

This repository provides a reproducible open-hardware pipeline, slicer optimization benchmark, and raw/processed dataset for 3D-printing **age-scaled pediatric upper-limb osteology Teaching-Learning Materials (TLM)**—**Scapula, Clavicle, Humerus, Ulna, and Radius**—on a desktop CoreXY FDM printer (**Creality K1**, `0.4 mm` nozzle, Klipper firmware, **OrcaSlicer `2.5.1`**).

### Key Contributions
1. **Parametric Pediatric Osteology Kit (Blender 5.2)**:
   - Watertight, single-shell (`0` non-manifold edges, `0` degenerate faces) anatomical bone meshes across `1:2` desktop, `1:1` adult, **5-year-old child (`190 mm` humerus)**, and **8–9-year-old child (`240 mm` humerus)** scales.
   - **Classroom Wire-Articulation DFAM**: Modeled `Ø2.4 mm` Z-axis wire tunnels (`12` holes across 5 bones) printed with `4` wall loops (`1.68 mm` solid sleeve) for `16–18 AWG` wire or elastic cord assembly without post-print drilling.
2. **Slicer Vertical-Shell Ablation**:
   - Automated G-code extrusion audit across 17 print jobs showing how disabling `ensure_vertical_shell_thickness` and `detect_narrow_internal_solid_infill` eliminates curved-bone "internal solid-infill bloat" (`>60%` → `1.0%–3.8%`) without flattening anatomical geometry.
3. **Build-Orientation Validation (`flat45` vs. `tilt40` vs. `split-pin`)**:
   - Empirical comparison on the `240 mm` Age 8–9 Humerus documenting `tilt40` bed-detachment failure (`< 4 mm²` contact area, `164 mm` lever arm) versus zero-support `split-pin` (`halfA`/`halfB` + `Ø1.85 mm` filament alignment pegs) and `45°` flat tree-supported plates.
